# SPPA Feature Groups

**Proposed specification · Document revision 0.1.0-draft.1 · 2026-10-04**

The primary SPPA feature model has eight groups:

**Capability → Policy → Runtime → Offer → Execution → Evidence → Evaluation → Reputation.**

The caller's **Decision Record** sits between comparison and execution. Historical
evidence also feeds future decisions. Groups organize responsibilities and contracts;
they are not eight separate transports, mandatory services or a ranking of trust.
The six-layer publication is retained as [design history](/sppa-protocol/spec/decision-layers/0.1.0-draft.1/).

This document revision is not a new Core wire release. Core 0.1.0/0.1.1 snapshots,
schemas and API remain unchanged. Proposed requirements use MUST/MUST NOT to describe
the intended model, not to claim released schemas or complete implementations.

## The eight groups

| Group | Question | Main information |
| --- | --- | --- |
| G1 Capability & Contract | Can it do this job? | Semantic capability/version, features, parameters, input/output roles, guarantees and declared limits |
| G2 Policy & Eligibility | May this caller use it for this job? | Privacy, license/use rights, visibility, location/residency, permissions and approval |
| G3 Runtime & Availability | Is it ready now? | Scoped health/liveness, capacity, queue, load, availability and admission |
| G4 Offer & Economics | What are the terms for these inputs? | Typed prices, total/capped charges, wait/runtime/completion estimates and expiry |
| G5 Execution, Artifact & Reuse | What is happening and where is the result? | Jobs, progress, artifact access, revision/session state and authorized reusable candidates |
| G6 Evidence & Validation | What facts and checks support the outcome? | Receipts, hashes, actual measurements, provenance and deterministic validation |
| G7 Evaluation & Feedback | Does this output fit the goal? | Scoped rubric judgment, ratings, comments, suggestions and responses |
| G8 Reputation & Learning | What can the next caller learn? | Workload/version-specific historical outcomes, prediction accuracy, samples and uncertainty |

G1–G4 organize current decision context. G5–G8 organize work and accumulated experience.
These are not a compulsory temporal sequence: reuse is considered before selecting
a new execution; historical evidence and reviews can inform the first comparison.
Policy belongs explicitly to G2 but MUST be enforced across every group.

## Common record boundaries

Records retain provider/capability/configuration scope, authenticated issuer, object
identity/revision, observation/publication times, applicable expiry/window, evidence
references and limitations. Source classes remain distinct: provider declaration,
observer measurement, deterministic check, AI judgment and aggregate.

Access filtering precedes disclosure. Knowing an ID or hash grants no permission.
Visibility does not grant invocation or artifact access. Do not expose another
principal's job, private matching artifact, rate bucket or review through discovery.
Every dynamic value needs source and freshness at the field or shared observation
level; a heartbeat and queue measured at different times cannot share a fabricated
new timestamp. Receivers preserve unknown, undisclosed, stale and non-comparable states.

## G1 Capability and Contract

Separate the shared semantic definition from provider configuration. Specify result
meaning, version, supported features, parameter schema, semantic input/output roles,
limits and guarantees. Declared concurrency/queue/rate/timeout/retry limits are not
current free capacity. Configuration changes bind to a revision; changing load does
not change a capability's semantic version.

Matching IDs or MIME types alone MUST NOT imply substitutability or composability.
A caller checks required meaning, features, version and constraints. For example,
`audio.mix` input and `audio.stems` output describe roles beyond a generic audio MIME.

Current coverage: [Core discovery](/sppa-protocol/spec/0.1.1/discovery/) and
[execution declarations](/sppa-protocol/spec/0.1.1/admission/). Full shared semantic
contracts remain proposed.

## G2 Policy and Eligibility

Capability fit and authorized use are independent. Required confidentiality,
training/logging/retention, execution/storage regions, rights, permissions, delegated
spending authority and approvals are hard conditions, not preferences to average.

Each requirement returns **PASS, FAIL or UNKNOWN**, with reason, source and evidence.
UNKNOWN MUST NOT satisfy a hard condition. If data must stay in Thailand and storage
location is undisclosed, automated invocation stops until the uncertainty is resolved.
Distinguish execution region from storage, backups, logs and onward processing.

A fictional stem-separation provider may fit perfectly, be available and have strong
quality evidence, yet explicitly forbid the required commercial use. The caller
excludes it before ranking. This example makes no licensing claim about a real tool.

Implementation, model, input, output and service-hosting rights are separate. Wrapper
license metadata does not grant underlying rights. Provider declarations and trusted
verification remain distinguishable. Check identity/visibility before returning
records, and recheck authorization at admission, transfer, revision and reuse.

Current coverage: [Core policy](/sppa-protocol/spec/0.1.1/policy/) implements its declared
privacy/permission constraints. Full rights/offer eligibility contracts remain proposed.

## G3 Runtime and Availability

Keep observer-scoped reachability, subject-scoped heartbeat, service health and
admission distinct. A responsive gateway does not certify a GPU worker. A healthy
provider with all slots occupied may still accept queued work. Public load excludes
private caller quotas and identifying job details.

Snapshots bind source, provider/capability/configuration, observation/expiry, receiver
receipt and ordering where supported. Check arithmetic against declared configuration.
Expired or unavailable evidence becomes unknown or unusable for readiness; missing
values are not zero. Heartbeats MUST NOT extend queue, offer or progress freshness.

The [experimental runtime profile](/sppa-protocol/design/runtime-status/) defines scoped
heartbeat checks, epochs, sequence gaps and full resync. Cold-start/availability models
may inform offers when evidenced; they are not assumed to be implemented. Queue depth
alone is not an ETA. Native heartbeat publishing/subscriptions remain planned.

## G4 Offer and Economics

An immutable offer binds issuer, provider/capability/configuration, input facts/digests,
parameters, policy/rights, terms revision and expiry to one job or authorized reuse
candidate. Changed inputs or terms require re-evaluation.

Use exact decimal amounts, currency, named billing units, quantity basis, increments,
minimums and all applicable charges. `input_media_minute`, `compute_minute` and
`gpu_minute` are different units. Compare normalized job totals, preserving original
values. Missing costs are unknown, not free. Actual charges require measured usage
and authoritative billing evidence; this repository does not implement payment.

A hard budget requires a valid enforceable maximum covering applicable charges, not
an estimate below the limit. Price information grants no payment authority. Budget
reservation, payment hold and compute reservation are separate mechanisms.

Timing separates queue wait, cold start/setup, runtime and transfer. For example,
42 seconds waiting plus 80 seconds running is a 122-second subtotal, not a full
completion guarantee when setup/transfer remain unknown. Declare method, workload,
units and uncertainty. Quote expiry governs acceptance of terms, not necessarily
completion of the job. Offers reserve no slot; final admission stays with the provider.

Current coverage: [proposed Decision Contract](/sppa-protocol/design/decision-contract/).
Core has no complete offer, capped billing or payment implementation.

## Caller-owned Decision Record

Decision is not a ninth feature group or a provider's claim. The main AI produces
a record under trusted caller authority after considering current G1–G4 and applicable
historical G6–G8 evidence or G5 reuse candidates. Hub returns candidates and evidence;
it MUST NOT silently substitute its own winner or priorities.

The proposed record identifies decision/caller, goal/work reference, selected provider
and offer revision, relevant input/configuration bindings, hard requirements and their
outcomes, soft priorities, evidence references/timestamps, exclusions/unknowns and
stable reason codes. Capture an actionable rationale; do not require hidden reasoning.

```yaml
# Conceptual example; not an accepted Core message or released schema.
decision:
  selected_provider: provider-b
  selected_offer: offer-b-revision-3
  hard_requirements:
    privacy: confidential
    commercial_use: allowed
    maximum_total: {amount: "0.05", currency: USD}
  eligibility: PASS
  priorities: [completion_time, total_cost]
  reason_codes: [within_cost_ceiling, permitted_commercial_use, shorter_estimated_completion]
```

Privacy and required rights MUST NOT become soft weights. Validate the offer/evidence
again when it expires or configuration changes. A Decision Record does not authorize
new side effects, artifact access or money movement, and reserves no compute. Binding
correlation and durable Decision Record schemas remain to be specified.

## G5 Execution, Artifact and Reuse

Providers own atomic admission, queueing and execution. Preserve scoped idempotency
after an uncertain invocation; do not create fresh logical work blindly. Monitor
caller-private state and cancel/reconcile under explicit authority and deadlines.
Progress is measured or unknown, never inferred as a fabricated percentage.

Core job states remain queued, running, completed, failed, canceled and expired.
`awaiting_review`, current/accepted pointers, revision, branching and rollback belong
to future negotiated session/review profiles, not retroactive Core state changes.
Producing a candidate is not accepting it; accepting a preview does not validate final
delivery. Bound iterations, time and spending; prevent concurrent pointer overwrites.

Artifacts carry immutable identity, semantic role, integrity, lineage, ownership,
retention and transferable rights. Authorized transfer moves bytes separately from
control messages. A reference is not a public URL or permission grant.

Reuse is a first-class decision candidate, not merely internal caching. Check exact
input/parameter/contract/configuration fingerprint, tenant/access scope, rights,
freshness, provenance and nondeterminism policy before revealing a match. Compare
valid reuse terms against fresh execution. Reuse may have transfer, storage or license
charges; it is not inherently free or instantaneous. Request idempotency is not
cross-job result reuse. Session/reuse/composition families remain planned.

Current coverage: [jobs](/sppa-protocol/spec/0.1.1/jobs/) and
[artifacts/transfer](/sppa-protocol/spec/0.1.1/artifacts/).

## G6 Evidence and Validation

Record actual execution facts, job/input/parameter/output bindings, build/configuration,
timing, measured usage when available, receipt and provenance. Preserve prediction
and observation separately. An FFmpeg zero exit code does not establish visual quality.

Validation identifies checker/version, requirements, measured values, output revision,
PASS/FAIL/UNKNOWN and evidence. Code can check integrity, codec, resolution, audio
presence and file readability; a check proves only its stated properties. A signature
authenticates the signer's assertion, not independent truth or goal fit. Provider
claims, caller observations and independent checks retain separate source classes.

Current coverage: [Core receipts](/sppa-protocol/spec/0.1.1/security/) and integrity
checks. A general validation contract and actual-cost evidence family are planned.

## G7 Evaluation and Feedback

Evaluate usefulness and goal fit using a named rubric/version, scale, workload,
evaluator identity, authorized run/result, evidence and uncertainty. Incompatible
rubrics are non-comparable. Another AI's opinion is not automatically more trustworthy
than a measurement or provider statement. Insufficient evidence stays unknown.

Keep rating, comment, structured suggestion, provider response and version-linked
resolution distinct. “Return FPS in output metadata” can become a tracked improvement
with a tested fix. Record revisions/disputes so feedback is not double counted.
Evaluation, acceptance and payment authority remain separate decisions.

Current coverage: proposed only; no released evaluation/feedback family.

## G8 Reputation and Learning

Summarize eligible historical execution, validation and evaluation under a disclosed
method. Scope provider, capability, version/configuration, workload and time window.
Keep success, validation pass, quality, ETA/cost prediction accuracy and downstream
composition success separate. Define each denominator and what constitutes success.

Report samples, unresolved outcomes, exclusions, confidence method, source mix,
correlated reviewers, selection bias and disputes. Deduplicate evidence and prevent
provider self-reports from looking independent. A new provider has unknown history,
not perfect quality. Model/configuration changes do not inherit unrelated scores
silently. Reputation cannot refresh runtime or override a failed eligibility gate.

Learning means documented experience informs later choices; it does not require
training an LLM or authorize reuse of confidential data for training. Aggregation,
feedback access and training permission remain separate. Formal reputation is planned.

## The feedback loop

```text
Capability -> Policy -> Runtime -> Offer
                         |
                 MAIN AI DECISION
                         |
Execution / Artifact / Reuse
             -> Evidence -> Evaluation -> Reputation
                                             |
                                 next decision's context
```

Only authorized, appropriately scoped records enter that context. Trusted runtime
code handles freshness, ordering and ordinary heartbeats. Main AI receives material
changes requiring a decision, not every telemetry event. The loop gives future callers
more context, not a guarantee that any individual judgment is correct.

## One example and acceptance gates

Illustrative confidential commercial video conversion, queueing allowed, hard ceiling
USD 0.05: G1 declares H.264 and four concurrent jobs; G2 confirms required permissions
and rights; G3 reports all slots occupied with queue room; G4 offers an enforceable
all-charge ceiling USD 0.05 with scoped estimates. The caller records its selection.
G5 runs the job and returns an authorized artifact. G6 measures timing and format;
G7 evaluates goal fit. G8's prior window reports 97 completed out of 100 resolved runs,
plus five unresolved runs. Those figures are examples, not collected results.

Before claiming full implementation, publish reviewed schemas/fixtures for every
implemented profile and test unknown rights, configuration drift, stale/gapped runtime,
expired offers, different billing units, unknown charges, denied artifact/reuse access,
uncertain execution replay, session concurrency, invalid receipts, partial validation,
non-comparable rubrics, duplicate feedback and unresolved aggregate outcomes.
The private M0–M2 plan remains the release boundary; this document authorizes no launch.

## Relationship to previous classifications

The earlier six assertion layers are design history; their distinctions between
claims, measurements, judgments and aggregates remain important. The seven decision
dimensions (Technical, Operational, Quality, Economic, Policy, Trust, Reuse) describe
subjects within records. Neither taxonomy adds extra primary feature groups.
For example, ETA is an Operational dimension in G4, while measured runtime is evidence
in G6. The 16-category feature catalog is detailed navigation across this eight-group
model, not a competing top-level protocol architecture.
